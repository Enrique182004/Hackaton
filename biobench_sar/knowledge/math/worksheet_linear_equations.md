# Worksheet: Linear equations

Solve each equation for x and write every step on the board. The problems get harder as you go: one-step
equations first, then two-step equations, then the distributive property, like terms and x on both sides.
The levels match the quiz bank's, so a quiz from both files still goes from easy to hard.

For the tutor: the Answer lines are only for checking the student's work. Never read an answer out loud;
explain the next operation instead.

## Level 2: one-step equations

1. x + 9 = 15
Answer: x = 6

2. x - 6 = 2
Answer: x = 8

3. 8x = 56
Answer: x = 7

4. x/5 = 4
Answer: x = 20

## Level 3: two-step equations

5. 2x + 3 = 11
Answer: x = 4

6. 6x - 5 = 25
Answer: x = 5

7. x/2 - 4 = 3
Answer: x = 14

8. 10 - 3x = 4
Answer: x = 2

## Level 4: brackets, like terms and x on both sides

9. 4(x - 1) = 20
Answer: x = 6

10. 3x + 2x + 4 = 29
Answer: x = 5

11. 5x - 3 = 2x + 9
Answer: x = 4

12. 2(x + 5) = 3x + 4
Answer: x = 6
